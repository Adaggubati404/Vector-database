This note is a recipe for how to bake sourdough bread.

To bake sourdough bread, start with a ripe starter. Mix the starter with flour and water until the dough comes together. Let the dough rest, then stretch and fold it. Those folds build strength in the sourdough dough. Dust the bench with flour so the dough does not stick while you shape it.

Shape the sourdough loaf and let it rise in a basket. Heat a dutch oven and bake the sourdough bread covered, then uncovered until the crust is deep brown. Cool the loaf before you slice it. Steam from the covered bake is what opens the ear on the loaf.

People bake sourdough bread because the starter gives the crumb its tang. A fresh slice of sourdough bread with butter is the reward for the wait. If the dough feels slack, give it another fold before you shape the loaf. The goal of this note is simple: bake sourdough bread, from starter to browned crust.
